Get Lumen

Free. Small download. Works on Windows 10 and 11.

Download Lumen
  1. Unzip Lumen.zip anywhere you like.
  2. Double-click Open Lumen.bat. The first time it sets itself up in a few seconds.
  3. Lumen downloads ffmpeg once, a free video tool it records with (about 90 MB).
  4. Play a game and press F9.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no account, no ads.

Will it slow my games down?

Lumen records with your graphics card's built-in video encoder, so most games barely notice. On PCs without one it falls back to the processor, which is heavier.

Where are my clips saved?

In Videos\Lumen. You can pick another folder in Settings.

Can I take my voice out of a clip?

Yes. Your mic is saved as its own track. Click Turn mic off and only game sound plays. Changed your mind? Turn it back on. You can also rename clips.

If you hear your own voice in your headset (mic monitoring or sidetone), your voice also ends up in the game sound. Turn that off and Turn mic off works fully.

Does Lumen track me?

Lumen sends one tiny note when it opens and when you save a clip: a random number for your PC and the app version. No name, no clips, no screen. It only counts how many people use Lumen. Turn it off in Settings.

Lumen is new. What if something breaks?

Lumen is a small personal project, so tell me. Lumen checks for updates by itself and installs them in one click.
